# We3 Brand Guidelines (`we3.live`)

## Brand Identity

* **Name:** We3 (Pronounced *"we three"*)
* **Domain:** `we3.live`
* **Core Tagline:** You, Me, and AI — In This Together.

## Brand Philosophy

Traditional software forces humans to do the mechanical work. Generic AI chatbots attempt to replace humans entirely with hallucinated responses. 

**We3** represents the golden middle: **The Customer, The AI, and You.** 

AI acts as infinite, frictionless middle-layer labor, while human operators maintain 100% strategic authority.

## Voice and Tone

* **Confident & Decisive:** We open with answers, not fluff.
* **Empathetic & Direct:** We validate operational reality while cutting out unnecessary noise.
* **Professional & Sharp:** Clean engineering aesthetics, enterprise reliability, zero gimmicks.
